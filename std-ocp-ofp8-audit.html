OCP OFP8 Revision 1.1 audit

Audit of 2026-11-14, against the requirements, on origin/main 8c7d47e (after PR #55, merge 6a18042: the fp8_e5m2 dtype and its CPU conversion, SRS v0.4.6; PRs #53 and #49 change no OFP8 code). History: first audited 2026-11-13 on ccf02b3 (E4M3 only), re-audited the same day on b99720a after PRs #48, #50 (scope narrowed to saturating conversion) and #52 (OFP8-2 fixed). PR #55 added the saturating field to the shared MiniFloat row and moved every line of numeric.rs below it; the E4M3 results below are unchanged and their line numbers are those of 8c7d47e.

Method. Read the specification text (pdftotext -layout of specs/number-formats/ocp-ofp8-rev1.1.pdf): §3, §4.2, §5.1 with Tables 1 and 2, §5.2.1 with Table 3, and Appendix A. Read origin/main at 8c7d47e: crates/rustinfer-target/src/numeric.rs, src/cpu/codec.rs, src/reference/mx.rs, src/reference/kv.rs, crates/rustinfer-mir/src/dtype.rs, crates/rustinfer-kc/src/dialect/types.rs and src/mlir/lower.rs; the tests crates/rustinfer-target/tests/numeric.rs, tests/mx.rs, tests/kv.rs, tests/e5m2.rs, the E5M2 golden generator tests/e5m2_golden/mod.rs and its output tests/data/e5m2_golden.txt, and crates/rustinfer-mir/tests/e5m2.rs. Read SRS v0.4.8 RI-MIR-003 (docs/SRS-RI-v0.1.md:591-634) and the state of PR #44 (open). No tests were run.

Test tags. tests/numeric.rs and tests/e5m2.rs tag Verifies RI-MIR-003 and Verifies RI-TGT-003; crates/rustinfer-mir/tests/e5m2.rs tags Verifies RI-MIR-003. The E4M3 cases in tests/mx.rs and the MX golden file are tagged Groundwork for RI-MIR-012, because RI-MIR-012 is not complete (#36, #38); their assertions still bind the E4M3 encoder, which they call.

Independent checks. E4M3: test_every_fp8_pattern_round_trips (tests/numeric.rs:141) decodes all 256 patterns and requires every non-NaN one to encode back to itself; since the encoder never yields 0xFF or 0x7F from a number, this also proves that both NaN patterns decode to NaN. The MX golden generator (tests/mx_golden/mod.rs:66-77) builds the E4M3 grid from the bit fields with integers only, and the 26 E4M3 golden lines must match the encoder bit for bit (test_golden_blocks_quantize_bit_exactly, tests/mx.rs:320). E5M2: the generator tests/e5m2_golden/mod.rs shares no code with numeric.rs and uses no floating point (every value an integer count of 2^−16 or 2^−17 units, every f32 assembled from its bits, :1-17). It writes a decode line for each of the 256 codes and, for every pair of adjacent finite codes and for 57344 and the next binade's 65536, the midpoint with the f32 one ULP either side, both signs (midpoints, :123-141), plus the clamp boundary, f32::MAX, ±Inf, ±0 and the smallest f32 subnormal (boundaries, :144-153): 758 round lines. test_golden_file_is_the_independent_generator_output (tests/e5m2.rs:87) requires the committed file to be its output.

E5M2 (OFP8-R12 to R18), new since PR #55

The dtype is DType::Fp8E5m2, token fp8_e5m2, one byte, quantized (crates/rustinfer-mir/src/dtype.rs:30-35, :74-81, :141-151); it is left out of FLOAT_OR_QUANTIZED, so no matmul or KV write reads it (:84-94), as RI-MIR-003 says nothing in R1 does. The conversion is the shared MiniFloat encoder with the row MiniFloat::E5M2 (crates/rustinfer-target/src/numeric.rs:78-88), reached through Fp8Format::E5m2 (:253-295) and the CPU codec (src/cpu/codec.rs:67, :80).

item requirement result evidence
OFP8-R12 1-5-2 layout, bias 15, emax 15, emin −14 conforms MiniFloat::E5M2: 5 exponent bits, 2 mantissa bits, bias 15 (numeric.rs:80-88); sign bit 7 (:90-92); min_exponent −14 (:103-105). test_fp8_e5m2_token_and_element_size (crates/rustinfer-mir/tests/e5m2.rs:68): element size 1
OFP8-R13 normal and subnormal value formulas conforms the shared decoder (numeric.rs:127-132). test_every_code_decodes_to_the_golden_value (tests/e5m2.rs:100): all 256 codes equal the generator's integer-built values bit for bit
OFP8-R14 Inf S.11111.00, NaN S.11111.{01,10,11}, zeros, max ±57344, min normal 2^−14, max subnormal 0.75 × 2^−14, min subnormal 2^−16 conforms ieee_specials: true makes the all-ones exponent Inf at mantissa 0 and NaN otherwise, either sign (numeric.rs:84, :143-150); max is exponent 30 with mantissa 3 (:108-110), E5M2_MAX 57344 (:27). test_table_2_values (tests/e5m2.rs:137) asserts every Table 2 row by value, both signs of max and of the three NaN codes; test_format_maxima (numeric.rs:367)
OFP8-R15 from binary32, binary16, bfloat16 with roundTiesToEven conforms round (numeric.rs:176-183); the codec widens bf16 and fp16 exactly before the single rounding (cpu/codec.rs:60-83). test_every_golden_rounding_case_encodes_to_the_golden_code (tests/e5m2.rs:120): every midpoint goes to the even code, one f32 ULP either side to the near code, both signs, subnormals included; test_every_code_round_trips (:202)
OFP8-R16 NaN gives a NaN; ±Inf gives ±57344 (saturating) conforms a NaN encodes to 0x7E, S.11111.10 (numeric.rs:25, :156-158); ±Inf passes round (:177-179) and reaches overflow, which with saturating: true returns the maximum, not the Inf pattern (:86, :185-192). test_nan_input_gives_a_nan_code (tests/e5m2.rs:176): either sign and a signalling payload; test_saturation_clamps_to_57344 (:156): ±Inf give 0x7B and 0xFB, and round_to_fp8 agrees
OFP8-R17 overflow after rounding gives ±57344 (saturating); otherwise the rounded value, the sign kept conforms numeric.rs:161-172, a negative result rounding to zero keeps the sign (:166-167). test_saturation_clamps_to_57344 (tests/e5m2.rs:156): 57344 + 1 ULP, 65536, f32::MAX, both signs; test_negative_zero_keeps_its_sign (:187): −0 is 0x80 and decodes to −0, −2^−18 and the tie −2^−17 give 0x80; the golden boundary lines (e5m2_golden/mod.rs:144-153)
OFP8-R18 57344 < x < 61440 rounds to 57344; 61440 rounds up and saturates (Appendix A) conforms ties to even at the top binade: 0x7B has an odd mantissa, so the tie at 61440 goes up to 65536, above max, and saturates (numeric.rs:176-183, :161-164). The generator's last midpoint pair is 57344 and 65536 with saturation (e5m2_golden/mod.rs:123-141), giving 0x7B one ULP below, at and above 61440; test_saturation_clamps_to_57344 asserts 61440 itself (tests/e5m2.rs:161, :166-167)

Beyond the clause's conversion: test_fp8_format_of_e5m2 (tests/e5m2.rs:219) maps the dtype to the format, and test_cpu_quantize_and_dequantize_launches_use_e5m2 (:226) runs the CPU quantize and dequantize kernels on fp8_e5m2 (per channel amax to 57344, back exactly). In the Model IR, an E5M2 tensor without a linear encoding is refused (crates/rustinfer-mir/tests/e5m2.rs:102), only quantize outputs and dequantize inputs admit it (:123, :167), and matmul and a KV group refuse it (:147, :204). Every acceptance line of RI-MIR-003 for fp8_e5m2 (docs/SRS-RI-v0.1.md:624-628) has its test.

Other users of the formats on origin/main

  • The Kernel IR's max identity for f8E4M3FN is 0xFE, −448, the most negative finite E4M3 value (crates/rustinfer-kc/src/mlir/lower.rs:23), asserted by test_max_identity_of_ocp_e4m3_is_minus_448 (lower.rs:441) (OFP8-2, fixed 2026-11-13). The Kernel IR also names f8E5M2 (crates/rustinfer-kc/src/dialect/types.rs:21-22) with max identity 0xFC, −Inf (lower.rs:24), correct since E5M2 has infinities (Table 2); no kernel of R1 uses it.
  • The fp8 KV CPU reference (PR #48, e218b8a) writes each K and V value as f32_to_fp8(x / scale, format) (crates/rustinfer-target/src/reference/kv.rs:277) and reads it back as bf16(scale × fp8(code)), so it inherits R4, R5 and R7 to R11; test_cached_k_and_v_equal_the_oracle_conversion_bit_exactly (tests/kv.rs:212) covers OCP E4M3 storage (tests/kv.rs:51). Since PR #49 (8c7d47e) the frontend can fill the per-layer scales of that KV group from a checkpoint's KV scale tensors (crates/rustinfer-frontend/src/kv_scales.rs:102-121); the stored E4M3 conversion is unchanged.
  • MxElement::E4m3 encodes with the same function (reference/mx.rs:109), so MXFP8 elements inherit the saturating behaviour.
  • E5M2 is not a checkpoint dtype: the safetensors reader has no F8_E5M2 entry (crates/rustinfer-artifact/src/safetensors.rs:56-67), so an E5M2 weight is refused as an unknown dtype. That is by design: SRS v0.4.6 adds no "KV dtype, kernel or checkpoint dtype" (docs/SRS-RI-v0.1.md:29).

Requirements of the SRS not yet on origin/main

SRS requirement what needs E4M3 where it is
RI-KC-003 OCP fp8 MFMA operands on gfx950 PR #44 (issue #8), open
RI-KVC-008 --kv-dtype fp8: E4M3 KV on gfx950 and cpu, written by the cache-write kernel CPU reference merged (PR #48, e218b8a); GPU kernels issue #38
RI-MIR-012, RI-CLI-002 --activations mxfp8: in-kernel E4M3 activation quantization on gfx950 issue #38, no PR

These are recorded as OFP8-3: the conversions they perform must meet R5 to R10, and bit-exact agreement with the CPU reference is their acceptance test. No requirement needs E5M2 on a GPU in R1.

Result

The E4M3 encoding (R1 to R3) and the E5M2 encoding (R12 to R14), and saturating conversion to both (R4 to R11 and R15 to R18, in the saturating-only scope of SRS v0.4.3), conform and are tested; E5M2 against an independent integer-only golden table of all 256 codes and 758 rounding cases. The non-saturating mode (OFP8-1) is out of scope. The one open gap is coverage: the gfx950 fp8 MFMA (PR #44) and the GPU KV and mxfp8 kernels (#38) are not on origin/main (OFP8-3). Partial, for the R1 coverage, not for any wrong value.
