AMDGPU DMA Operations (LLVM 23.1)

Scope (SRS §1.4, LLVM-AMDGPU-DMA): "for RI-KC-015, the per-lane global-to-LDS load widths (1, 2 or 4 bytes; 12 or 16 on gfx950) and the gfx1250 tensor load and store (llvm.amdgcn.tensor.load.to.lds, four descriptor groups on gfx1250, the fifth reserved)". Added by SRS v0.4.11 (PR #65, ADR-011). RI-KC-015 (i) requires each target to report its asynchronous tile movers (none, per-lane direct-to-shared loads with their byte widths, or a descriptor-based tensor mover with its maximum tile rank) and its lowering to map the Kernel IR's asynchronous tile copy (RI-KC-002) to one of them. R1 covers cpu, gfx942 and gfx950; the tensor-mover lowering ships "with the first target that has one (gfx1250; section 2.4, 'AMD next')", which has no release tag yet.

Implementation on main (b057ef5): none. The capability description (crates/rustinfer-kc/src/capabilities.rs:47-64, PR #63) has no mover field, the Kernel IR has no asynchronous copy (crates/rustinfer-kc/src/dialect/ops.rs:8-21), and no lowering emits a load.to.lds, global.load.lds or tensor.load.to.lds. Planned in issue #64 (open, R1): the mover field with the widths of this document, the copy and its wait, and the ROCDL lowering for gfx942 and gfx950; for gfx1250, the field and a synthetic capability set only.

Conformance: mandatory from R1 (RI-KC-015).

Source: AMDGPU DMA Operations, LLVM 23.1.0 documentation, https://releases.llvm.org/23.1.0/docs/AMDGPUDMAOperations.html ("Last updated on 2026-08-25"), downloaded 2026-11-15 with its Markdown source as specs/compiler/llvm-23.1.0-AMDGPUDMAOperations.md (146 lines; cited by line) and the page it refers to for completion tracking, AMDGPU Async Operations, as specs/compiler/llvm-23.1.0-AMDGPUAsyncOperations.rst. The operations as the pinned MLIR exposes them are read from specs/compiler/mlir-23.1.0/ROCDLOps.td and AMDGPUOps.td.
