AMDGPU intrinsics tracker

Gaps found by the audit of 2026-11-15 against main 5a6db49. AIN-1 and AIN-2 are R1 and wait on the tile distribution of #64 (open PR #77) and the capability fields of #81. AIN-3 is R2; its issue #105 sits on the milestone R1 — foundation only because no R2 milestone exists yet (the Epic #1 checklist lists it under "later releases ... not R1 gates").

id gap required by status notes
AIN-1 No llvm.amdgcn.wave.reduce.* and no llvm.call_intrinsic path: row reductions are lane-local vector.reductions because a tile is held whole by every lane (AIN-R1, AIN-R3) [TD] lines 2493 to 2510; SRS RI-KC-020 and its Acceptance ("reduce within a wave by cross-lane exchanges only and across waves through shared memory") Open Planned in #83 (cross-lane reductions and broadcasts), blocked by #64 and #81. On 5a6db49: crates/rustinfer-kc/src/mlir/rocdl/helpers.rs:504-562. The first llvm.call_intrinsic arrives with PR #77 (emit.rs:387 at 56d4382, implicitarg.ptr). wave.reduce gives one value per wave; rows narrower than a wave need the masked exchanges of AIN-R2 (see the audit)
AIN-2 No llvm.amdgcn.fdot2c.f32.bf16 (nor rocdl.fdot2.f32.bf16): no dot-product lowering and no vector-unit capability to gate it to gfx950 (AIN-R1, AIN-R4, AIN-R5) [TD] lines 3309 to 3340; SRS RI-KC-021 ("a length-K dot product of one row MAY use a native dot product ... a compiler setting the tuner measures") and its Acceptance (v_dot2_f32_bf16 in the gfx950 code object) Open Planned in #84 (vector-unit selection), blocked by #64 and #81. Not verified: which instruction the c intrinsic selects; the op-backed fdot2.f32.bf16 selects v_dot2c_f32_bf16 without modifiers and v_dot2_f32_bf16 otherwise (audit)
AIN-3 No llvm.amdgcn.s.memrealtime or s.getreg for per-work-group timestamps and die ids (AIN-R6, AIN-R7) [TD] lines 2242 to 2247, 2800 to 2802; SRS RI-OBS-006 Later release R2 #105 (device timestamps and compute-die ids per work group, R2)

Read from the rustinfer tree at commit 53af206 2026-11-19, branch detached of github.com/itej89/rustinfer. Every claim here was read from the source unless a page says otherwise. GPU results are the owner's hardware runs, cited by issue (#54); this site itself runs nothing.
