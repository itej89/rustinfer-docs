HIP and ROCm environment variables 7.2.4

Scope (SRS §1.4, HIP-ENV): "the variables RI-ROC-015 records, their meaning and defaults". Added by SRS v0.5 (PR #80). RI-ROC-015 (R1): "The version manifest (RI-CLI-010) SHALL record the HIP and HSA environment settings that change copy, queue, memory or launch behaviour, set or unset: HSA_ENABLE_SDMA, HSA_ENABLE_PEER_SDMA, HSA_FORCE_SDMA_SIZE, HSA_XNACK, HSA_CU_MASK, ROC_GLOBAL_CU_MASK, GPU_MAX_HW_QUEUES, HIP_FORCE_DEV_KERNARG, HIP_HOST_COHERENT, GPU_FORCE_BLIT_COPY_SIZE, ROC_P2P_SDMA_SIZE, AMD_SERIALIZE_KERNEL, AMD_SERIALIZE_COPY. A value that differs from the runtime default SHALL be logged at warn. rustinfer SHALL NOT set any of them itself." RI-OBS-005 copies the same environment into every tune and GPU test record (see AMD SMI).

Implementation on main (5a6db49): none of the 13 is read, recorded or logged; the version manifest holds the software, toolchain, host and dependency lines, the HIP library path and, at device open, the HIP runtime version (crates/rustinfer-core/src/manifest.rs:36-136; crates/rustinfer-target-rocm/src/lib.rs:150). rustinfer sets none of them: the only std::env::set_var calls in the tree are in a test of the HIP library override (crates/rustinfer-target-rocm/tests/hip_override.rs:94-125). Planned in issue #95 (open, R1) for the ROCm target, and #14 for the print on every subcommand.

Three of the 13 are not in the documents. HSA_FORCE_SDMA_SIZE, GPU_FORCE_BLIT_COPY_SIZE and ROC_P2P_SDMA_SIZE appear in neither the HIP nor the ROCm reference of 7.2.4; their meaning and defaults are read from the ROCm source at tag rocm-7.2.4 (ROCM-SRC, informative), see the requirements.

Conformance: mandatory from R1 (RI-ROC-015).

Source: HIP environment variables, https://rocm.docs.amd.com/projects/HIP/en/docs-7.2.4/reference/env_variables.html, and ROCm environment variables, https://rocm.docs.amd.com/en/docs-7.2.4/reference/env-variables.html, both downloaded 2026-11-15 into specs/runtime/ as hip-7.2.4-env_variables.html and rocm-7.2.4-env-variables.html with text copies (.txt, cited by section and line). For the three undocumented variables and the runtime's own defaults: projects/clr/rocclr/utils/flags.hpp and projects/rocr-runtime/runtime/hsa-runtime/core/util/flag.h of ROCm/rocm-systems at tag rocm-7.2.4, as specs/runtime/rocm-7.2.4-clr-flags.hpp and rocm-7.2.4-rocr-flag.h.
