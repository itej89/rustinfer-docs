# IEEE 754-2019 tracker

Gaps found by the [audit](std-ieee-754-audit.html).

No gap is open. Every in-scope item conforms on `origin/main` (`ccf02b3`).

One limit is recorded here and not counted as a gap: the audit used
secondary sources only, because IEEE Std 754-2019 is paywalled. If a copy
becomes available, check IEEE-R1 to IEEE-R9 against its clauses and add the
clause numbers to the [requirements](std-ieee-754-requirements.html).
