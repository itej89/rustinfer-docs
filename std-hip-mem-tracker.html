HIP memory management tracker

Gaps found by the audit of 2026-11-15 against main 5a6db49. HMEM-1 to HMEM-3 are R1 (RI-ROC-008, RI-WST-006) and planned in #93 (blocked by #92 for the alignment only) and #94 (blocked by #93), both open. PR #110 (#93, opened 2026-11-15 21:47 UTC, during this audit) closes HMEM-1 and HMEM-2 on its head 82c9ab3. It merged on 2026-11-19 (bf1129c), which closes them on main; their gfx950 run is GPU test #114. HMEM-4 and HMEM-5 are R2; their issues #104 and

100 sit on the milestone R1 — foundation only because no R2 milestone

exists yet (the Epic #1 checklist lists them under later releases).
