FIPS 180-4

Scope (SRS §1.4, FIPS-180-4): SHA-256 only. Conformance: mandatory R1. The requirements that cite it are RI-ART-001 (the artifact header's hash of every following byte), RI-ART-005 (the optional per-tensor digest), RI-ART-008 and RI-SEC-004 (the checkpoint identity: config.json, every safetensors header and, since SRS v0.4.7, every weight byte range the build used), RI-ART-012 (the identity check on every load) and RI-WST-005 (Sluice's --verify-weights).

Implementation: SHA-256 is not written by rustinfer. It comes from the RustCrypto sha2 crate, pinned exactly (Cargo.toml:55, sha2 = "=0.10.9"), behind one type, rustinfer_artifact::Sha256 (crates/rustinfer-artifact/src/hash.rs:20-64): Sha256::of hashes a byte slice (hash.rs:35-39) and the digest is carried in JSON as 64 lowercase hex digits (hash.rs:41-63). Call sites on origin/main (8c7d47e): the writer and reader of the artifact hash (writer.rs:110, reader.rs:288), the safetensors header identity (safetensors.rs:277, metadata.rs:147-152), the config.json hash (crates/rustinfer-frontend/src/lowering.rs:221, crates/rustinfer-sluice/src/checkpoint.rs:124-133), the used weight ranges of the identity (PRs #53 and #49: recorded by the build with Sha256::of over the range's bytes, crates/rustinfer-frontend/src/kv_scales.rs:57-71, and re-hashed at load by feeding sha2::Sha256 in 64 KiB chunks, crates/rustinfer-sluice/src/checkpoint.rs:100-121) and Sluice's streamed per-tensor digest, which feeds sha2::Sha256 chunk by chunk (crates/rustinfer-sluice/src/sluice.rs:265-280).

Source: specs/data/fips-180-4.pdf (NIST, FIPS PUB 180-4, Secure Hash Standard (SHS), August 2015, doi:10.6028/NIST.FIPS.180-4, https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/fips/180-4/upd1/final). Free.

Algorithm (§5, §6.2) conforms, through sha2 0.10.9; checked by the "abc", empty, two-block, padding-boundary and one-million-a vectors
Digest form (§3.1, §6.2.2) conforms: 256 bits, lowercase hex
Used-range digests conform today; the build's digest and the load's are two code paths agreeing only while a used range is at most 4 bytes, and the chunked path is untested past one chunk (SHA256-2)
Open gaps 1 (SHA256-2; SHA256-1 fixed in b99720a)

rustinfer does not claim FIPS validation. The standard's announcement says "Only algorithm implementations that are validated by NIST will be considered as complying with this standard" (item 8); sha2 is not a validated module, and nothing in the SRS asks for one. The claim is the algorithm and its output, nothing more.
