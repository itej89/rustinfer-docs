AMDGPU intrinsics (LLVM 23.1)

Scope (SRS §1.4, LLVM-AMDGPU-INTRINSICS): "the AMDGPU intrinsics the lowering emits through llvm.call_intrinsic because MLIR 23.1 has no op for them: fdot2c.f32.bf16 and wave.reduce.* (R1, RI-KC-020, RI-KC-021), s.memrealtime and s.getreg (R2, RI-OBS-006)". Added by SRS v0.5 (PR #80, from the AMD feature inventory AMD-INV, row SC-07 and the cross-lane and vector-unit rows). RI-KC-020 lowers the reductions of a distributed tile to the target's cross-lane exchanges; RI-KC-021 selects the vector-unit forms, among them a native dot product for a length-K row; RI-OBS-006 (R2) records a constant-rate timestamp and the compute die per work group.

Implementation on main (5a6db49): none. No crate emits llvm.call_intrinsic, and none of the four intrinsics appears in the tree. That follows from the lowering of R1 so far: a tile is a value every lane holds whole (crates/rustinfer-kc/src/mlir/rocdl/helpers.rs:4-22), so a reduction is a lane-local vector.reduction per row (:504-555) and there is nothing to exchange across lanes. Distributing tiles over lanes is #64 (RI-KC-019 (a), open PR #77); the cross-lane reductions are #83, the vector-unit selection #84, the per-work-group timestamps #105 (R2).

Conformance: mandatory from R1 (RI-KC-020, RI-KC-021); the timer intrinsics are R2 (RI-OBS-006).

Source: llvm/include/llvm/IR/IntrinsicsAMDGPU.td at tag llvmorg-23.1.0, downloaded 2026-11-15 from https://raw.githubusercontent.com/llvm/llvm-project/llvmorg-23.1.0/llvm/include/llvm/IR/IntrinsicsAMDGPU.td as specs/compiler/llvm-23.1.0-IntrinsicsAMDGPU.td (4295 lines; cited by line). Read with it, at the same tag: MLIR's LLVMOps.td (the llvm.call_intrinsic op) and the pinned ROCDLOps.td and AMDGPUOps.td (which ops exist), and, to see what an intrinsic becomes on gfx942 and gfx950, the AMDGPU target's AMDGPU.td, VOP2Instructions.td, VOP3PInstructions.td, VOP3Instructions.td, SIInstructions.td and SIISelLowering.cpp, all under specs/compiler/.
