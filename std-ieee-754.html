IEEE 754-2019

Scope (SRS §1.4, IEEE-754): binary16 and binary32 encodings, NaN and Inf, roundTiesToEven conversion. The requirements that cite it are RI-MIR-003 (fp16 is binary16 and fp32 is binary32; the CPU reference converts with roundTiesToEven) and RI-TGT-003 (the oracle's fp32 is binary32 and its conversions round ties to even).

Implementation: the CPU reference in rustinfer-target.

  • fp32 is Rust's f32, which is binary32. The dtype is declared in crates/rustinfer-mir/src/dtype.rs:21-23 ("IEEE single").
  • fp16 is declared in crates/rustinfer-mir/src/dtype.rs:18-20 ("IEEE half"). Its codec is crates/rustinfer-target/src/numeric.rs: the format row MiniFloat::FP16 (:41-48), decode (:96-128), encode with roundTiesToEven and overflow to infinity (:131-168), and the public f32_to_fp16 and fp16_to_f32 (:286-294).
  • The operand codec of the CPU target, crates/rustinfer-target/src/cpu/codec.rs:60-81, decodes every float dtype exactly to f32 and encodes results with these functions.

bf16 is not an IEEE 754 format. The SRS defines it as binary32 with the low 16 mantissa bits dropped; f32_to_bf16 (numeric.rs:268-276) rounds ties to even and keeps NaN a NaN. It is listed in the audit as a related check only.

Conformance: mandatory from R1.

Source: IEEE Std 754-2019, IEEE Standard for Floating-Point Arithmetic, published 2019-07-22, https://standards.ieee.org/ieee/754/6210/. The standard is paywalled and was not obtained. The requirements are taken only from freely available secondary sources, each marked as such: the IEEE SA abstract page (specs/number-formats/ieee754-abstract.html), the IEEE 754 working group's public background notes (specs/number-formats/ieee754-conformance-and-options.txt), LLVM APFloat at llvmorg-23.1.0 (specs/number-formats/llvm-23.1.0-APFloat.h and .cpp), the definitions of OCP MX v1.1 §4.1 and OCP OFP8 Revision 1.1 §4, and the FP16 row of Table 30 of the AMD CDNA3 ISA guide. All downloaded 2026-11-13.
